What I've built
Malawi Forecasts
Data Engineer & Full Stack · 2025
The friction
Farmers in rural Malawi were guessing planting seasons. Climate data existed, but only as fragmented files, no one could turn them into a “plant this now” recommendation.
The system
A pipeline that ingests 5 years of climate records, engineers seasonal features, then runs a decision tree classifier that explains its recommendations in plain language. Offline dashboards + CSV exports for field use without internet.
The outcome
Planners now use 5‑year forecasts to guide crop choices. The system is simple enough to trust, flexible enough to adapt.

Philosophy

Data without context is noise. Systems without purpose are just infrastructure.
